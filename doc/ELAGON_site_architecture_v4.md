# Elagon Website Architecture + Copy System v4.0

**Status:** Implemented working direction
**Scope:** Homepage, Services, Work, case detail, Playbook, Company, Contact
**Strategic source:** Elagon Strategic Baseline, Website Copy Deck v3.0, Flagship Case Studies
**Reference use:** Distyl technology and case-study information architecture; no visual or verbal imitation

## 1. One story for the whole site

### Category

**Production AI for complex operations.**

### Core promise

**AI that works. Value that lasts.**

### Plain-language explanation

Elagon redesigns important work and builds production AI around the people, data and decisions that make it work.

### Story in six moves

1. Begin with one critical workflow.
2. Define one measurable operating change.
3. Learn how the work really happens.
4. Build the complete system around the model.
5. Keep accountable people in control.
6. Establish how the system will run and improve.

This replaces three competing stories—“AI-native transformation,” “production AI,” and “client ownership”—with one causal story: **important workflow → working system → measurable result → lasting operating ability.**

## 2. Page responsibilities

| Page | Its only job | It should not repeat |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Establish the promise, show proof, reveal the path | Full methodology, operating-mode detail, long company claims |
| Services | Explain what a client can buy, when to enter and what decision each engagement produces | Full Playbook standards or broad brand manifesto |
| Work | Prove that Elagon changes real operations | Service descriptions or generic process claims |
| Case detail | Show one causal chain from constraint to system to qualified result | Portfolio-wide positioning or sales-page language |
| Playbook | Explain how Elagon makes production delivery dependable | Pricing, engagement durations and service packaging |
| Company | Explain who Elagon is, how the senior team works and who fits | Detailed service deliverables or case narratives |
| Contact | Qualify one useful first conversation | Another explanation of the full company |

## 5. Services

### Page thesis

- Hero: **Start with one workflow.**
- Intro: Choose a material operating problem. Prove the assumptions that matter. Build only when the evidence supports it.

### Engagement 01 — AI Workflow Sprint

- Editorial title: **Choose the workflow**
- Promise: Make one informed production decision in ten business days.
- Decision: Is this the right production opportunity?
- Outputs: current and redesigned workflow; value case and baseline; production concept and validation plan

### Engagement 02 — Production Validation

- Editorial title: **Prove the path**
- Promise: Test what could make the investment fail before committing to a full build.
- Decision: Does the evidence justify production?
- Outputs: working solution slice; evaluation and user evidence; refined business case and implementation plan

### Engagement 03 — Production Build + Launch

- Editorial title: **Put it to work**
- Promise: Build the minimum complete system required for dependable operation.
- Decision: Is the system ready to operate and transition?
- Outputs: production system; integrations, controls and evaluation evidence; runbooks, training and transition package

### After launch

Show the choice once, only on Services:

- Client-operated
- Co-managed
- Elagon-operated

The copy must always state that the client retains business-process, policy, data and outcome accountability in all three modes.

## 7. Case-detail template

Every case follows the same evidence order:

1. **Outcome-led hero** — public result, not a vague capability title
2. **Impact strip** — two or three qualified operating measures
3. **The constraint** — what was getting in the way
4. **The intervention** — how the work was redesigned
5. **The operating system** — visible workflow sequence
6. **What Elagon built** — software, AI, integration and controls
7. **Human control** — who reviews, decides, escalates and improves
8. **What moved into the operation** — concrete delivered capability
9. **Measurement note** — qualifier and evidence boundary
10. **Related work + CTA**

This borrows the clarity of the Distyl pattern—impact, challenge, solution, capability—while adding the elements that carry Elagon’s differentiation: workflow redesign, human control, operating ownership and evidence qualification.

## 8. The Elagon Playbook

### Page thesis

- Hero: **The work around the model.**
- Intro: A repeatable operating method for turning one material problem into a dependable system that people can govern, use and improve.

### Six decision gates

1. Frame the problem — Is it material and measurable?
2. Learn the work — Do we understand how it really happens?
3. Redesign the workflow — Is the future workflow viable and accountable?
4. Prove the risk — Does the evidence justify production?
5. Build the system — Does it meet the agreed production standard?
6. Establish ownership — Can it be run responsibly after launch?

### Four non-negotiables

- Value is explicit
- Judgment has a place
- Performance is tested
- Ownership starts early

### Closing idea

**The system should keep working without hidden dependency.**

## 9. Company

### Page thesis

- Hero: **The people who design it stay accountable for it.**
- Role: a senior, cross-functional practice for established enterprises and PE-backed companies with complex operations

### Story order

1. Why Elagon exists: the distance between AI ambition and dependable operation

3. How Elagon shows up: Advisor, Builder, Operating partner
4. Who fits: material workflow, complex judgment, accountable owner, access to real conditions

Avoid a generic “capabilities grid.” Skills only matter as evidence that the team can hold the full thread from business result to production operation.

## 11. Language system

### Voice

- Plain, specific and calm
- Short headline; one supporting thought
- Business noun before AI noun
- Observable change before technical mechanism
- Confident without claiming inevitability

### Preferred constructions

- One critical workflow. One measurable change.
- Find the value. Prove the path. Put it to work.
- The model is only one part.
- The outcome is the story.
- The people who know the work stay in control of it.

### Avoid

- “Transform your business with AI”
- “Unlock the power of AI”
- “End-to-end AI transformation”
- “Revolutionary,” “seamless,” “cutting-edge,” “next-generation”
- Repeating “capability,” “production-ready” or “ownership” in every section
- Using “agentic” when the business meaning is simply a controlled workflow

### CTA inventory

- Primary: **Discuss a workflow**
- Work: **Read case study**
- Services: **See the engagement**
- Playbook: **Explore the Playbook**

## 12. Visual + motion unity

The established design direction remains the source of truth:

- classical-modern, image-led editorial composition
- cream whitespace; moss as the grounded brand field; rust as a climax; blue as computational contrast
- large serif statements after the sans-serif Hero exception
- one dominant idea per viewport
- minimal labels and no decorative feature-card language
- image and headline composed as one field
- motion carries the Homepage; subpages prioritize reading rhythm
- motion reveals hierarchy or workflow state, never adds continuous decoration
- reduced-motion users receive the complete static composition

The Distyl reference influences information hierarchy—clear problem, system and impact—not Elagon’s visual language. Elagon should remain warmer, more editorial, more human and more explicit about how accountable expertise stays inside the system.
